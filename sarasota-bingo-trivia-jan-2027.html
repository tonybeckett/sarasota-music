Weekly rhythm

Monday: Good Liquid trivia, Florida Provisions bingo, and Venice trivia at the British Open Pub and Back Porch. Tuesday is the deepest night, with Music Bingo at The Peculiar Pub, bingo at Good Liquid and Big Top, and trivia at Brewster’s, Mellow Mushroom, Tamiami Tap and Ed’s Tavern. Wednesday adds Bingo For All, Clancy’s bingo, Holy Cross parish bingo in Palmetto, and music trivia at Ed’s Tavern. Thursday has bingo at The Peculiar Pub, Florida Provisions and Dubz BBQ, plus Quiz & Tonic at The Westin. Sunday is bingo at Encore Live, Rocco’s in Bradenton, and the Knights of Columbus hall in Venice. Sahib Shrine’s public bingo runs Tuesday daytime. New or newly sourced regulars include Moosic Bingo at Venice Moose Lodge (Mondays; members and guests), Music Bingo at I’Lunie’s in Palmetto (Tuesdays), IACV Bingo in Venice (1st and 3rd Wednesdays), and Trivia at Loaded Cannon Distillery (Thursdays).
