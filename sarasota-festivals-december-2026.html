Updated Wednesday, November 25, 2026, 9:05 AM ET

Summary

December around Sarasota is packed with parades, boat parades, holiday markets and light displays, especially the first two weekends of the month. Here are the standouts:

  • Opening weekend: Friday, Dec 4, brings the Holiday Night of Lights on St. Armands Circle (tree lighting at 6:30 PM) and the first night of Wellen Wonderland in Venice. Saturday, Dec 5, stacks the Downtown Sarasota Holiday Parade, North Port’s Poinsettia Parade & Festival, Bradenton’s Winter Wonderland, Palmetto’s Christmas in the Park, and the start of Lakewood Ranch’s Holiday of the Arts.
  • Holiday lights: Selby Gardens’ Lights in Bloom runs select nights Dec 5–23 and 26–30 (closed Dec 24, 25 and 31), with early entry at 5:30 PM. Venice’s VABI arboretum lights glow nightly through Jan 1, with a special Santa evening on Dec 12. Palma Sola Botanical Park’s Winter Nights Under the Lights is Dec 17–20. The Ringling hosts Holiday Splendor on Dec 10 and 17 and Holiday Nights on the Bayfront on Dec 29–30.
  • Boat parades: The Venice Christmas Boat Parade is Saturday, Dec 5, at 6 PM. Sarasota’s 40th annual Sarasota Holiday Boat Parade of Lights is Saturday, Dec 12, 6:30–8:30 PM, free to watch from Bayfront Park. The Manatee River Holiday Boat Parade is also Dec 12 (start time not posted on the official homepage).
  • Markets and art shows: The 20th Atomic Holiday Bazaar is Dec 12–13 at Robarts Arena ($6 adults). Also that weekend: Englewood’s Manta Market and Anna Maria Island’s Mistletoe Market (Dec 5–6). UTC’s Holiday Night Market is Dec 17. The Sarasota Fine Art Show returns to J.D. Hamel Park on Dec 19–20.
  • Community celebrations: Venice’s Grand Chanukah Klezmer Festival is Dec 6 (free). Saturday, Dec 12, has Christmas in Cortez, Jingle Jam at CoolToday Park and A Night Before Christmas at G.T. Bray Park. Anna Maria’s Lester Family Fun Day is Dec 5. Lakewood Ranch’s Christmas on Main is Dec 19–20.
  • New Year’s Eve: Downtown Sarasota’s Pineapple Drop starts at 1 PM, with the pineapple drop at midnight and fireworks over the bay. New Year’s Eve at Waterside Place (7 PM–midnight, drone show) and New Year’s Eve at UTC (7–9 PM, fireworks) are free family options.

Christmas Eve and Christmas Day are quiet on this list: Lights in Bloom is closed, and no other festivals are confirmed those days. See holiday notes for Hanukkah, Christmas and New Year’s Eve, and for two events whose 2026 dates were not confirmed.
