January is a full calendar: 139 listings at 51 venues and market sites across ten areas — Longboat Key, Anna Maria Island, Bradenton & Palmetto, Lakewood Ranch, UTC, Sarasota, Siesta Key, Venice, Nokomis & South Venice, North Port and Englewood. Farmers markets make up 76 of those rows; the other 63 are wine tastings and dinners, cooking classes, food festivals, seafood festivals, chili cookoffs and food trucks. Every day of the month has at least one listing except Thursday, January 7.

Highlights

The month peaks with Forks & Corks, now in its 20th year. The official dates are January 21–25, and this page lists 29 festival rows: 23 winemaker dinners (January 19–26, about $120–$250), 4 Forks & Corks University master classes on Saturday, January 23 at Michael’s On East ($69–$89), the Open House at Michael’s Wine Cellar that afternoon ($25), and the Grand Tasting at The Ringling on Sunday, January 24. The Grand Tasting is sold out, as are 7 of the dinners, according to the organizers’ pages. Dinners not marked sold out include Arts & Central (Jan 22, $135), Mademoiselle Paris (UTC) (Jan 22, $160) and Café Venice (Jan 25, $120). The festival’s own dates differ between sources; see the notes.

Two free BBQ & Seafood festivals bracket the month: the Sarasota Seafood & Music Festival at Sarasota Fairgrounds (January 8–10) and the Coquina Beach Seafood & Music Festival in Bradenton Beach (January 22–24). Chili has its own weekend: the Venice Chili Champion Cookoff at Hotel Venezia on January 9 (free entry, tasting tickets $2–$20) and Shellback’s Englewood Chili Cookoff at Pioneer Plaza (Englewood) on January 23 (free).

Smaller tastings run through the month: Sarasota Art Museum’s Uncorked with Orin Swift wines on January 12 ($58 members / $68 non-members), Michael’s’s Wine Cellar Social Club on January 14 ($56) and La Norma’s Italian wine tasting lunch on January 30 ($150). Chef Mike’s hands-on Latin and sushi classes (January 6 and 20, $140 with meal, beer and wine) and five $69 classes at STC Sarasota fill the cooking slate. BellaVino’s Journey Around the World tasting on January 12 is sold out.

Don’t miss, and the busy days

The busiest days are Friday, January 22 and Saturday, January 23, with 14 listings each, followed by Saturday, January 9 (9), which stacks the seafood festival, the Venice chili cookoff, the Main Street Market in Lakewood Ranch and the Saturday markets. January 22 has nine Forks & Corks dinners plus the BellaVino All White Party; January 23 adds four master classes, the Open House and two more dinners. Sunday, January 24 holds the Grand Tasting, the Coquina Beach festival’s last day and the Meadows Farmers Market.

Free and low-cost

Weekly and monthly farmers markets (76 listings) are free to walk into: Saturdays in downtown Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice and North Port; Sundays at Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Wellen Park and Mixon Farms; Wednesday at Phillippi Estate Park; Friday at Nathan Benderson Park; Tuesday on Anna Maria Island; and Wednesday, Friday and Sunday at the Coquina Beach Market (closed January 1, 22 and 24). Monthly markets are the Lakewood Ranch Main Street Market (January 9), RADD Sunday Market (January 10) and Meadows Farmers Market (January 24). Ranch Nite Wednesdays at Waterside Place is free with rotating food trucks. The Forks & Corks Open House is $25, and the master classes range from $69 to $89.

Still to be listed

There is no January restaurant week: Savor Sarasota is June 1–14, 2027. Nothing turned up for Dry January, Epiphany or king cake, or for a barbecue cook-off in the area. The North Jetty Food Truck Rally has no January date, and Sarasota Wine Co.’s weekly tastings have no posted January dates, so they are not listed. Prices are blank on 9 listings where official pages did not state them. Forks & Corks retail tastings other than the Michael’s Open House are still “TBA.”

Conflicts and cautions

Where sources disagreed, the table uses the venue or organizer’s own page when one exists. Confirm details before you go.

  • Forks & Corks 2027 — festival dates — The festival’s main page and ticket site say January 21–25, 2027 (winemaker dinners Jan 21–25, retail tastings Jan 22–23, master classes Jan 23, Grand Tasting Jan 24, trade tasting Jan 25, online auction Jan 18–24). SRQ.travel gives the festival as January 18–25. The Originals’ winemaker-dinner page is headed January 20–27, and its list runs from a Clasico dinner on Jan 20 to a Masō dinner on Jan 26. Individual dinners are listed here on the dates their own pages give, including Jan 19 (Mademoiselle Paris), Jan 20 (Clasico) and Jan 26 (Masō).
  • Forks & Corks winemaker dinners — prices — Michael’s Wine Cellar’s page lists the Robert Craig dinner (Jan 21, Michael’s On East), the Chappellet dinner (Jan 22, Michael’s On East) and the Bordeaux dinner (Jan 23, at the Wine Cellar) at $195 per person including gratuity; Venice Wine Experiences shows $145 for two of them. The Originals’ calendar shows all three as sold out without a price. Fins at Sharky’s shows $192.60 on the Originals’ calendar and $150 on Venice Wine Experiences. Mean Dean’s shows $150 on the Originals’ calendar and $125 plus tax and gratuity on its own page. Both prices are shown in the table. Most other dinner prices come from the Originals’ calendar and match Venice Wine Experiences where both list one.
  • Forks & Corks — Michael’s On East and Mean Dean’s (Jan 22) — The Originals’ summary page swaps the locations for “Mean Dean’s & Torii Mor” and “Michael’s On East & Chappellet.” Its own calendar, Mean Dean’s page, and Michael’s page agree that Torii Mor is at Mean Dean’s Local Kitchen (Bradenton) and Chappellet is at Michael’s On East (Sarasota). The table follows those.
  • Forks & Corks Bordeaux dinner (Jan 23) — venue — Michael’s Wine Cellar’s own page, its Eventbrite listing and a Facebook event place the Château Phélan Ségur / Canon-La-Gaffelière / d’Aiguilhe dinner at the Wine Cellar, 1283 S Tamiami Trail. The Originals’ event page lists Michael’s On East, 1212 S East Ave. The table follows the Wine Cellar’s own page, Eventbrite and Facebook.
  • Mademoiselle Paris — Jan 22 dinner — The Originals’ summary lists the Lafon-Rochet / Pédesclaux / Lilian Ladouys dinner only as “Mademoiselle Paris.” Its calendar entry and the restaurant’s page put it at UTC (8527 Cooper Creek Blvd). Mademoiselle Paris’s Bordeaux Prestige series also runs Jan 19 (Main Street), Jan 20 (Gulfport, outside this coverage area) and Jan 21 (Anna Maria). The Jan 19 and Jan 21 dinners are on the restaurant’s page only (Venice Wine Experiences also lists Jan 19).
  • Mademoiselle Paris — French Riviera dinner (not January) — Venice Wine Experiences lists a French Riviera wine dinner on Jan 26 and Jan 28. The restaurant’s own pages date that series Feb 23–26, 2027 ($99), so it is left out of January.
  • BellaVino — Venice Winter All White Party (Jan 22) — BellaVino’s own page lists a $10 ticket, DJ Ryan McMahon, and Moët & Chandon Champagne as the featured drinks, 6–9 PM. Venice Wine Experiences lists it as a “Champagne & Caviar” event at $125 plus tax. The table uses both prices.

The only prices found are in Venice Wine Experiences ($225 general / $425 early / $550 VIP, plus fees). Noon–4 PM is also from that listing; the official site gives the entry times only.
