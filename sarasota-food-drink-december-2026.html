December is a full calendar: 151 listings at 67 venues and market sites across ten areas — Longboat Key, Anna Maria Island, Bradenton & Palmetto, Lakewood Ranch, UTC, Sarasota, Siesta Key, Venice, Nokomis & South Venice, North Port and Englewood. Farmers markets make up 69 of those rows; the other 82 are wine and spirits tastings, dinners, cooking classes, food truck gatherings, holiday meals and festivals. Every day of the month has at least one listing except Christmas Eve (December 24).

Highlights

Wine, Beer & Spirits leads the ticketed events (39), from the Collefrisio Italian wine-pairing dinner at Alma de España on December 1 ($125) and Arts & Central’s Taste of France dinner on December 2 ($149.80) to a Champagne dinner with Champagne Lallier at Sarasota Wine Co. on December 3 ($200). Mademoiselle Paris hosts the same Noël à Paris Champagne and Bordeaux dinner at its Main Street, UTC and Anna Maria rooms (December 8, 10 and 11, $119). Michael’s Wine Cellar holds its Open House and Holiday Toy Drive on December 5 ($25 plus an unwrapped toy) and the Annual Holiday Champagne Tasting on December 16 ($95+). Sarasota Art Museum’s Uncorked: Holiday Bubbles is December 8 ($58 members / $68 non-members), a six-wine tasting with cheeses; the museum’s current page and Eventbrite agree on that format and price.

Food festivals include the European Holiday Market at the Bradenton Area Convention Center (December 2–6, $5 advance / $10 at the door), the Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key’s Lawn Party by the Bay at Ken Thompson Park on December 5 ($100), the 15th annual Taste of Chanukah Food & Music Festival at Phillippi Estate Park on December 6 ($7 before November 30, then $10), and the 8th Annual International Crafts & Food Festival at the Islamic Society of Sarasota and Bradenton on December 12 (10 AM–5 PM). Cooking classes (6) run from a free Nutrition Cooking Class at the North Sarasota Library on December 8 to Chef Mike’s Thai class ($140), VistaCucina’s Christmas pizza and lasagna sessions ($100), a holiday grazing-boards class at STC Sarasota on December 14 ($69 — full, waitlist only), and a children’s cookie-decorating workshop at Young Chefs Academy on December 21. Food trucks turn up at the North Jetty Food Truck Rally (December 4 and 18, free admission) and at Ranch Nite Wednesdays at Waterside Place. Holiday food (19) covers Lakewood Ranch Santa breakfasts and lunches, brunch with Santa at Tzeva (December 6) and Arts & Central (December 13), GROVE’s buffet breakfast with Santa (December 6, $55 / $35), gingerbread or dreidel decorating at the Ritz-Carlton (December 13 and 20, $145++ per party of four), Breakfast with the Grinch on December 23, and the Ritz-Carlton’s Holiday Grand Ballroom Brunch on December 25 ($185 / $60). The only BBQ & Seafood event posted is the New Year’s Eve Oyster Drop on Bridge Street in Bradenton Beach (December 31).

Don’t miss, and the busy days

The first week, December 1–6, has 46 listings. Saturday, December 12 is the peak day (14), followed by Saturday, December 5 (13) and Sunday, December 6 (11). December 5 stacks the Lawn Party, Michael’s toy-drive tasting, a Collefrisio tasting at Rumours Wine Bar, Santa lunches at The Peculiar Pub and Osteria 500, and the European Holiday Market. December 12 brings Christmas at the Sarasota Farmers Market, Good Liquid Brewing’s 5-year anniversary at Waterside Place, the International Crafts & Food Festival, an Italian wine tasting and lunch at La Norma on Longboat Key, Sips with Santa at Harry’s Continental Kitchens, and Lakewood Ranch’s monthly Main Street Market.

Free and low-cost

Weekly and monthly farmers markets (69 listings) are free to walk into, including Saturday markets in downtown Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice and North Port, Sunday markets at Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Wellen Park and Mixon Farms, and the Coquina Beach Market on Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays. The North Jetty Food Truck Rally is free admission. The Nutrition Cooking Class on December 8 is free, with registration required. The European Holiday Market is $5 in advance or $10 at the door; Taste of Chanukah is $7 before November 30, then $10. Brewing Ideas at Calusa Brewing is $5 plus fees, Cocktail Chronicles at The Lounge by Good Liquid is a $10 ticket (optional $35 flight), and ABC Fine Wine & Spirits’ premium tasting on December 3 is $15. Most Lakewood Ranch Santa meals do not list a price on the event pages.
