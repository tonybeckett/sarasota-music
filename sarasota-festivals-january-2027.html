Updated Wednesday, November 25, 2026, 9:20 AM ET

Summary

Summary

January around Sarasota opens with the last nights of the holiday lights, then turns to outdoor art festivals, two seafood-and-music weekends, the Manatee County Fair and Forks & Corks. Here are the standouts:

  • Holiday lights wrap up: Selby Gardens’ Lights in Bloom is open New Year’s Day and Saturday, Jan 2 — the last two nights (early entry 5:30 PM). Venice’s VABI arboretum lights glow from dusk through Jan 1 only.
  • Opening weekend: Saturday and Sunday, Jan 2–3, pair the Sarasota Fine Art Festival at J.D. Hamel Park with the Longboat Key Festival of the Arts at Town Center Green, both 10 AM–5 PM and free.
  • Seafood and music: The Sarasota Seafood & Music Festival is Jan 8–10 at the Sarasota Fairgrounds (Friday 4:30–10 PM, Saturday 11 AM–10 PM, Sunday 11 AM–6 PM; free admission). The Coquina Beach Seafood & Music Festival follows Jan 22–24 at Coquina Beach in Bradenton Beach.
  • Art shows: Hot Works’ Sarasota Fine Art Show is Jan 9–10 at St. Armands Circle. Venice-Nokomis Rotary’s Fine Arts & Crafts Festival is Jan 16–17 at the Airport Festival Grounds. The Anna Maria Winter Art & Craft Show is Jan 17–18 at City Field. That next weekend: the Lakewood Ranch Fine Art Festival and the Palmetto Chalk Art Festival (both Jan 23–24). The month closes with the St. Armands Circle Art Festival and the Downtown Venice Craft Festival on Jan 30–31.
  • The fair and MLK weekend: The Manatee County Fair runs Jan 14–24 in Palmetto (adults $10; Kids Day is Monday, Jan 18). Palmetto Youth Center hosts the MLK Banquet on Friday, Jan 15, at 6:30 PM and the MLK Parade on Saturday, Jan 16, at noon.
  • Food, wine and Englewood: The 20th Forks & Corks Food & Wine Festival is listed Jan 21–25, with the Grand Tasting at The Ringling on Sunday, Jan 24, 11 AM–4 PM (VIP and general admission were sold out when checked). Saturday, Jan 23, also brings Englewood’s Manta Market (9 AM–2 PM) and the Englewood Chili Cookoff at Pioneer Plaza (5–9 PM, free).

New Year’s Day is quiet except for the last light displays. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 18) has the fair’s Kids Day and the Anna Maria art show; the City of Sarasota’s Unity Walk had not been posted for 2027. See holiday notes and Check before you go.
