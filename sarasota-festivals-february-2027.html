Summary

February is peak festival season around Sarasota. Most of the action falls on three back-to-back weekends: Presidents’ Day weekend (Feb 13–14), Feb 19–21 and Feb 26–28. Here are the standouts:

  • Presidents’ Day & Valentine’s weekend: Saturday–Sunday, Feb 13–14, brings the 39th Downtown Sarasota Festival of the Arts on Main Street (200-plus artists, free), the 45th Cortez Commercial Fishing Festival (10 AM–6 PM, $5, kids under 12 free) and the Wellen Park Festival of the Arts in Venice. Englewood adds the Cracker Fair and the Manta Market on Saturday.
  • Biggest weekend, Feb 19–21: The free Bradenton Area River Regatta opens Friday night with a drone light show and fireworks at Rossi Park, then runs Formula 1 powerboat races on Saturday. The same weekend has Thunder By The Bay at the Sarasota Fairgrounds, the St. Barbara Greek Festival and the Longboat Key Festival of the Arts (Sat–Sun).
  • Food and drink: Venice has the Jewish Food Festival (Sun, Feb 7) and the Wine & Chocolate Walk (Thu, Feb 11, $10, 21 and over). Later in the month come the four-day Venice Italian Feast & Carnival (Feb 25–28), the free Englewood Seafood & Music Festival (Feb 26–28) and Anna Maria Island’s Taste of the Island (Feb 28).
  • Closing weekend, Feb 27–28: Pick from Paragon’s Sarasota Festival of the Arts at J.D. Hamel Park, the Springfest Art & Craft Show in Holmes Beach, Sarasota Audubon’s Fly Wild Festival (Sat), the American Vintage Market at Robarts Arena (Fri–Sat) and Englewood’s Beatles-Themed Family Festival (Sun).

Mardi Gras (Feb 9) is quiet this year: no local parade or festival was confirmed. See holiday notes for Mardi Gras, Valentine’s Day and Presidents’ Day, and Check before you go for four events with unconfirmed details.
