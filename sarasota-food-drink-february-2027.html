February is a full calendar: 128 listings at 42 venues and market sites across ten areas — Longboat Key, Anna Maria Island, Bradenton & Palmetto, Lakewood Ranch, UTC, Sarasota, Siesta Key, Venice, Nokomis & South Venice, North Port and Englewood. Farmers markets make up 74 of those rows; the other 54 are wine tastings and dinners, cooking classes, food festivals, BBQ and seafood events, a weekly food-truck night and Valentine’s food. Every day of the month has at least one listing, and the last weekend (February 25–28) is crowded with festivals.

Highlights

Wine, Beer & Spirits (16) runs from Michael’s Wine Cellar’s seated Wine Cellar Social Club on February 3 ($56) and its Saturday Open House on February 13 ($25, 50+ wines) to the Sarasota Art Museum’s Uncorked: Wines of Piedmont on February 9 ($58 members / $68), downtown Venice’s Wine & Chocolate Walk on February 11 ($10 wristband) and a Darioush wine dinner at Chart House on Longboat Key on February 25 ($189). Mademoiselle Paris hosts its $99 French Riviera wine dinner in three rooms: Sarasota on February 23, UTC on February 25 and Anna Maria on February 26. BellaVino’s Monday Sommelier’s Pick in Venice is listed on each Monday. New this pass: a Sip & Stroll vineyard tour to Fiorelli Winery on February 7 ($69.99) and the museum’s Unconventional Evening fundraising dinner on February 17 ($300).

Food festivals and BBQ & Seafood events carry the month. The month opens with the Jewish Food Festival in Venice (February 7), then the Cortez Commercial Fishing Festival (February 13–14, $5) and the City of Palmetto Rib & Mac-N-Cheese Cook-Off (February 13, no start time published) share the second weekend. The St. Barbara Greek Festival runs February 19–21 in Sarasota, the State College of Florida’s Bourbon, Brews & BBQ benefit is February 19 in Bradenton ($200), and the Kosher Food Festival is February 21 in Bradenton (free admission). The last weekend brings the Englewood Seafood & Music Festival (February 26–28, free admission), the Italian Feast & Carnival in Venice (February 25–28) and Taste of the Island in Holmes Beach (February 28, $1 tickets, plates $5–$15). Cooking classes (13) are mostly Suncoast Technical College’s $59–$69 hands-on classes, including a Valentine’s Dinner class on February 10, plus the $750 Cooking for Wishes charity dinner on February 25.

Don’t miss, and the busy days

Saturday, February 13 is the peak day with 11 listings: the Cortez Fishing Festival, the Palmetto cook-off, the Michael’s Open House, the Manatee Performing Arts Center’s Valentine’s dinner and show ($178.50), and a full slate of Saturday markets. It is followed by a five-way tie at 8 listings: February 6, February 7, February 14, February 27 and February 28. Valentine’s Day falls on Super Bowl Sunday (February 14), so the Manatee Performing Arts Center’s Valentine’s dinner and show on February 13 is the night before. No restaurant has posted a Valentine’s or Super Bowl menu yet, apart from a note on Sunnyside Café’s calendar.

Free and low-cost

The 74 farmers market rows are free to walk into, including Saturday markets in downtown Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port and Englewood, Sunday markets at Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Wellen Park and Mixon Farms, the Coquina Beach Market on Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays, and Englewood’s Thursday market. The Englewood Seafood & Music Festival and the Kosher Food Festival have free admission (food is extra). Ranch Nite Wednesdays at Waterside Place is a free food-truck night. Low-cost picks include the $10 Wine & Chocolate Walk, the Cortez festival at $5 (children under 12 free), the Italian Feast at $5 on Thursday and $10 other days, and Suncoast Technical College classes at $59–$69.
